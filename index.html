THE PHI PRINCIPLE

Nothing finished.
Everything refining.

In historical alchemy, these operations appeared in different traditions and sequences. AlchemyPhi uses one familiar seven-stage interpretation as an organizing framework, not as a universal historical canon.

Its systems and personal lenses are modern creative exercises. They can support reflection and iteration, but they do not establish chemical, psychological, or mystical truths.

Open by design. Private by default. Make something useful, then refine it again.
